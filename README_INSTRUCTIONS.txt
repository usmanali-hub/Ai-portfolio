HOW TO UPLOAD TO GITHUB:
=======================================
1. Open https://github.com/usmanali-hub/Ai-portfolio
2. Click 'Add file' -> 'Upload files'
3. Drag and drop all the files from this folder into GitHub:
   - index.html
   - usman-ali-portfolio.html
   - usman_ali_portrait.jpg
   - Resume.pdf
4. Click 'Commit changes' at the bottom.
5. Visit https://usmanali-hub.github.io/Ai-portfolio/ to see your updated portfolio!
